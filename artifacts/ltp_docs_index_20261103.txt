- [Account Assets](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/assets-v2-account-assets.md): Query Funding, Trading, DeFi and Earn account assets.

  - [Get Funding Account Assets](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-funding-account-assets.md): Returns Funding balances by currency and network.
  - [Get Trading Account List](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-trading-account-list-v2.md): Lists accessible DMA accounts and RapidX Portfolios.
  - [Get Trading Account Assets](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-trading-account-assets-v2.md): Returns the asset detail for one DMA account or RapidX Portfolio.
  - [Get Trading Asset Summary](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-trading-asset-summary-v2.md): Returns Trading assets aggregated by currency.
  - [Get Trading Asset Distribution](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-trading-asset-distribution-v2.md): Returns Trading currency distribution across DMA accounts or RapidX Portfolios.
  - [Get DeFi Account Assets](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-defi-account-assets.md): Returns DeFi balances by currency and network.
  - [Get Earn Product Assets](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-earn-product-assets.md): Returns Earn holdings by product, including product identity, holding quantities and yield-related fields.

## API Reference: MarginX
- [Group Management](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/marginx-group-management.md)
  - [Get Margin Groups](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-margin-groups.md): Returns your MarginX margin groups with valuations, outstanding debt, borrowing capacity, LTV and MMR.
  - [Create Margin Group](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/create-margin-group.md): Creates a MarginX margin group with eligible DMA accounts and/or RapidX Portfolio venues.
  - [Get Margin Group Details](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-margin-group-details.md): Returns asset, debt, borrowing-capacity and risk data for one MarginX group, without a member list.
  - [Get Margin Group Members](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-margin-group-members.md): Returns paginated DMA and RapidX members with their asset and debt valuations, including zero-asset members.
  - [Update Margin Group](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/update-margin-group.md): Updates a MarginX group name and/or its complete member set. Requires Main User Level access with TRANSFER permission.
  - [Delete Margin Group](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/delete-margin-group.md): Deletes a MarginX group and releases its members without deleting the underlying accounts or moving funds. Requires TRANSFER permission.
- [Borrowing & Repayment](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/marginx-borrowing-repayment.md)
  - [Get Borrowing Currency Configuration](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-marginx-currency-config.md): Queries all MarginX borrowing-currency configurations, amount limits, floating-pool availability and floating/fixed rate terms. No query parameters.
  - [Get Margin Group Borrowable Assets](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-margin-group-borrowable-assets.md): Queries one currency’s group borrowing capacity, quantity limits, floating-pool availability and floating/fixed annual rates. Does not submit a loan.
  - [Borrow MarginX Funds](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/borrow-marginx.md): Submit a borrowing request to a group member. Main User Level TRANSFER required. LTP covers transaction fees; borrowing interest remains payable.
  - [Repay MarginX Debt](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/repay-marginx.md): Submit a repayment, interest first then principal. Main User Level TRANSFER required. LTP covers transaction fees; borrowing interest remains payable.
- [Debt & Activity](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/marginx-debt-history.md)
  - [Get Outstanding Debts](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-marginx-outstanding-debts.md): Lists unpaid MarginX principal and interest, including interest-only debts. Optional exact group filtering, pagination and valuation currency.
  - [Get Interest Accrual History](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-marginx-interest-history.md): Queries historical interest charges, calculation bases and applicable annual rates. FIXED does not necessarily mean fixed-term borrowing.
  - [Get Borrowing and Repayment Orders](https://apidocliquidity.readme.io/reference/get-marginx-borrow-and-repay-orders.md): Queries borrowing, principal-repayment and interest-repayment orders. Includes independent split-repayment records and historical borrowing.
